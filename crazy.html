Common Questions

Before
we work together.

Is this just a website service?
No. A website is one possible solution. We start with the bottleneck in your customer journey and recommend only the work needed to address it.
What if I only need a website, commercial or ads?
That's fine. The Customer Acquisition Sprint is the main framework, but individual projects such as conversion websites, campaign commercials, SEO or paid campaigns can be scoped separately when they solve a clear business problem.
How much does a project cost?
Project pricing depends on the bottleneck and scope. Founding projects are currently scoped from roughly ₦250k upward, with the exact investment agreed after the discovery call. Advertising spend is separate.
Do you guarantee customers or sales?
No. Customer acquisition depends on your offer, market, sales process, pricing and other factors outside our control. We do commit to diagnosing the problem clearly, building the agreed solution properly and using available data to improve it.
When do I pay?
For project work, a deposit is required before work begins. The remaining balance is due at the agreed milestone before final handover. Media spend and third-party costs are separate.
Who is Brandie Creative not a good fit for?
We are not the right fit for businesses looking for guaranteed sales, unpaid trial work, or a long list of unrelated deliverables for one small fee. We work best with businesses that have a real offer and are willing to invest in improving how customers find and choose them.
