Common Questions

Got
Questions?

How long does a typical project take?
Most projects are delivered in 3–6 weeks depending on scope. Landing pages can move faster — sometimes in 2 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline on our first call so there are no surprises.
Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?
Yes — Brandie Creative works with clients worldwide. We collaborate fully remotely and have delivered projects across Africa, Europe and North America. Time zones are never an obstacle.
What do I need to provide to get started?
Your goals and a rough budget or timeline. We'll handle strategy, design, motion and build — with or without existing brand assets.
Can I get just a motion graphics video without a website?
Absolutely. Motion graphics can be scoped as a standalone project — explainer videos, social promo content, logo animations, and product demos can all be delivered independently of a web project.
How many revisions are included?
Every plan includes 2 rounds of revisions. In practice, we do a thorough briefing process upfront so revisions are minimal and focused. Additional revision rounds can be added if needed.
What makes Brandie Creative different from other agencies?
We combine design and motion under one roof, with a sharp focus on conversion — not just looks. You get a designer who understands communication strategy, not just aesthetics, and who's invested in your results after launch.
